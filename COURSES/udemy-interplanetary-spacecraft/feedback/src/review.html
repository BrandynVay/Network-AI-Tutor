The four formulas worth carrying

F = ṁ × vₑ · thrust = mass thrown per second × exhaust speed
c = λ × f · wavelength × frequency = speed of light
P = flux × area × efficiency · solar array power
battery = load × shadow time ÷ depth of discharge

The one idea that repeats three times

Twice as far = a quarter as much. It is why gravity weakens with height (Section 1), why a radio signal fades (Section 5) and why sunlight fades (Section 7). Anything that spreads out over a sphere does this.

Recipe for Quiz 5: size a solar array

1. Sunlight per m² = 1,361 ÷ (distance in AU)².
2. Usable per m² = that × efficiency × any loss factor they give.
3. Area = power needed ÷ usable per m².
4. If there is a battery to refill, add its recharge power to "power needed" first.
Example: 500 W at Mars, 30% cells, no other losses. 1,361 ÷ 1.52² = 590. 590 × 0.30 = 177 W per m². 500 ÷ 177 = 2.8 m².
